Redefining Access to Quantitative Diagnostics

Quantiva DX is developing a new approach to point-of-care diagnostics—one designed to make quantitative biomarker information faster, simpler and more accessible.

Development-stage diagnostic technology

A result can be fast. 
A result can be informative.
We believe it should 
be both.

01 / SPEED

Move with the moment.

Diagnostic information is most useful when it arrives in time to influence the next decision.

02 / INFORMATION

Preserve what matters.

Speed should not mean reducing important biomarker information to a simple binary answer.

03 / ACCESS

Reduce the infrastructure.

We are designing compact formats that reduce reliance on complex laboratory systems and dedicated instrumentation.

Quantitative testing can be powerful. The systems around it can be demanding.

Important biomarker information is often tied to instrumentation, laboratory infrastructure, specialized workflows and time. Quantiva DX is exploring what changes when more of that complexity can be designed out.

InstrumentationDedicated analyzers can shape where testing can happen.
InfrastructureConventional workflows can require more space, systems and support.
WorkflowMore steps can increase the distance between sample and information.
AccessComplexity can limit how close meaningful measurement gets to the point of care.

 First development program

One biomarker.
A broader idea.

Our first development program focuses on C-reactive protein (CRP), translating an important inflammatory biomarker into a rapid, visual result using a compact card-based platform.

The CRP card is the first expression of the Quantiva DX philosophy: preserve meaningful information, simplify the system around it, and move that information closer to the point of care.

Rapid visual readout Compact card format Quantitative information
What we believe

Better diagnostic technology should do more than generate data. It should make meaningful information easier to use.

At Quantiva DX, that means making important biomarker information easier to access, easier to understand and available closer to the moment decisions are made.
